Brooklyn-based artist Zoe Schlacter is our latest collaborator and the inspiring creative behind a collection of limited edition sheets , throw blankets, and pillows in their signature maximalist style. Schlacter’s bold approach to pattern and color palette brings a bright spot and sense of play to all your coziest spaces. This collection combines art and real-world comfort to be enjoyed and not just admired.
